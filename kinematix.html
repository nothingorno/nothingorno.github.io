KinematiX Engine

Overview & Capabilities

KinematiX is a comprehensive Luau physics framework for Roblox Studio. It calculates true physical weights, structural stress points, aerodynamic drag, hydrodynamics, and thermal fatigue across models, allowing for high-fidelity destruction and simulation loops.
