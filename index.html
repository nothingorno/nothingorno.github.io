info Detailed Game Overview & Mechanics

Italian Railways is a simulation game created by Parthenope Studios, built around the scenic Ferrovia Faentina line running between Faenza and Florence. The simulator emphasizes prototypical accuracy, station control box mechanics, and cooperative multiplayer railway operations.

flag Station Masters (Capostazione)

Operate signal interlocking desks, adjust manual track switches, clear track blocks, and dispatch arriving trains using official signaling batons (Paletta).

confirmation_number Passengers & Railfans

Explore detailed station buildings, purchase and validate train tickets, board regional express trains, and take scenic photos along trackside view points.

traffic Italian Railway Signals & Regulations

Official RFI (Rete Ferroviaria Italiana) signal color meanings and operating procedures used in-game:

Rosso (Red - Stop)

Absolute stop signal. Train must come to a full stop before reaching the post.

0 km/h
Giallo (Yellow - Caution)

Advance warning. Prepare to stop at the next signal post.

30 - 60 km/h
Verde (Green - Clear)

Clear track ahead. Proceed at normal maximum line speed.

Line Speed
Giallo Lampeggiante (Flashing Yellow)

Upcoming track turnout switch set to divergent path.

Turnout Speed
